Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 October 2026

This policy describes how GeoTools handles information when you visit this website. GeoTools provides browser-based GIS and data-analysis utilities.

Information entered into the tools

The Distance Calculator, NDVI Calculator and Coordinate Converter perform calculations in your browser. The CSV Quick Stats tool reads the CSV file you select in your browser and calculates summaries locally. The current version is designed not to upload the coordinates, values or CSV contents you enter to a GeoTools application server.

Avoid entering confidential or sensitive information into online tools unless you have independently confirmed that the tool is appropriate for it.

Hosting and technical information

This website is hosted with GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information, such as IP address, browser details and request logs, to deliver and secure the service. Read GitHub's Privacy Statement for details.

Third-party services

The website currently imports fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may send technical request information to Google when loading these fonts. Read Google's Privacy Policy.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

As of the date above, GeoTools has not intentionally added Google Analytics or advertising code to this website. If analytics, advertising or other third-party services are introduced, this policy should be updated to describe them. If Google AdSense or another advertising service is enabled, that provider may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, personalise where permitted, and measure ads. Any consent choices required by applicable law will be provided before relevant technologies are used.

Your choices and policy updates

You can choose not to use the website. This policy will be updated if the site's features or third-party services change. For questions, use the feedback route on our Contact page; do not post personal or sensitive information in a public issue.
