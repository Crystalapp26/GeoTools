ABOUT GEOTOOLS

Built as a practical GIS learning space.

GeoTools is designed around a simple idea: GIS skills become more useful when you can experiment with them quickly. The site combines small geospatial calculators with data-analysis utilities in one clean workspace.

Use it for coursework, portfolio projects, quick checks, teaching demonstrations or just learning how common GIS calculations work.
