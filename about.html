ABOUT GEOTOOLS

Practical GIS tools for learning and analysis

GeoTools is a browser-based toolkit for students, GIS analysts, educators and anyone who wants to explore geographic and tabular data without installing specialist software.

What you can do

  • Distance Calculator: estimate the great-circle distance between two latitude/longitude points using the Haversine formula.
  • NDVI Calculator: calculate the Normalized Difference Vegetation Index from red and near-infrared reflectance values.
  • Coordinate Converter: convert decimal-degree coordinates to degrees, minutes and seconds.
  • CSV Quick Stats: inspect a CSV file's row and column counts and calculate simple numeric summaries in your browser.

Use results thoughtfully

These tools are intended for learning, quick checks and exploratory analysis, not as a replacement for validated GIS workflows or professional review. Results depend on the quality, units and suitability of your input data. NDVI interpretation also depends on the sensor, preprocessing and environmental context.

Privacy and feedback

The tools are designed to process their inputs locally in your browser. Website hosting and third-party services may still process technical information; see our Privacy Policy. To report an issue, visit our Contact page.
